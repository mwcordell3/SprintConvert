Soccer 30m Sprint Calculator

Estimate your soccer 30-meter sprint time, average speed, and a general competitive performance tier. Educational tool, not an official combine or scouting result.

The 30-meter sprint is a standard soccer speed test used in academy programs and physical-testing protocols around the world. It captures acceleration plus the early portion of max velocity, which mirrors the typical sprint demands of a soccer match.

This calculator estimates your 30m time and converts it to other sprint distances, with a general competitive tier from a cited reference. The 30-meter benchmark uses adult / teen male trained-player data — adjust your interpretation if you fall outside that group.

About the soccer 30m benchmark

The reference ranges shown are general performance tiers for trained competitive male players. Female-specific and youth-specific ranges are not yet included because we have not added strong cited sources. We would rather show no benchmark than show a misleading one.

Performance varies based on timing method, surface, footwear, wind, start type, and testing conditions.

Frequently asked questions

My team measures the 30y instead of the 30m. Will the calculator still work?

Yes. Choose 30 yards as the distance and the engine will still scale to other distances and use the soccer 30m benchmark for a directional comparison. The two distances differ by about 2.6 meters.

Does this work for women's soccer testing?

The calculator runs the math for any input, but the benchmark dataset for soccer 30m is currently sourced from a male-player reference. We chose not to publish a women's-specific tier without a strong cited source. Your conversions and speeds are still shown.

What is a competitive 30m soccer time?

Around 4.20 seconds is in the general 'competitive' range from our cited reference, with elite trained players running close to 3.70. These are broad ranges, not percentiles.
