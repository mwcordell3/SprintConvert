Privacy policy

Plain-language summary of how this site handles information.

Last updated: April 2026. This policy is starter language for an early-stage site and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before scaling.

Quick summary

  • All sprint calculations run in your browser. We do not send your inputs to a server.
  • No account is required. You cannot sign up or log in.
  • No email capture in v1.
  • No payment processing. No paid features.
  • No advertising or affiliate links in v1.
  • No analytics in v1. If we add analytics later, this policy will be updated first.

What we don't collect

  • We do not collect personal information in v1.
  • We do not ask for or store exact birthdays.
  • We do not ask for or store full names.
  • We do not ask for or store school names.
  • We do not ask for or store email addresses.
  • We do not collect health or injury data.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not sell personal information.

What stays in your browser

The calculator stores values you type only for the duration of your visit, in normal browser memory. We do not persist your inputs server-side. If you choose to share a result link, the inputs are encoded into the URL on your device and only travel where you send the link.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

This site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. To support athlete use without collecting personal data, we use broad age ranges only (13–14, 15–16, 17–18, college / adult) and do not ask for exact age, exact birthday, name, school, location, or email.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about how this site interacts with a minor, please use the contact page.

Changes to this policy

If we add analytics, change the data flow, or introduce email capture in a future version, this page will be updated before those changes go live. If we ever add affiliate links, the site will display a visible affiliate disclosure near those links and update this policy accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions, see the contact page.
