FAQ

Common sprint conversion questions

How accurate is SprintConvert?

It returns estimates, not official marks. Sprint performance is not linear, and timing method, start type, surface, and conditions all change real results. The explanation panel shows which inputs shaped the estimate.

What is the difference between hand time and FAT?

Hand-timed runs are typically 0.20 to 0.30 seconds faster than FAT because of human reaction delay starting the watch. The calculator can apply a +0.24-second adjustment to estimate a FAT equivalent for track-style hand times. For short combine-style tests, timing systems vary, so the adjustment is only an estimate.

Why does start type matter?

Block, 3-point, and standing starts all begin from rest. Rolling and flying starts skip the acceleration phase, so a flying 40 will look much faster than the same effort from a standing or 3-point start. Mixing start types reduces reliability in cross-distance comparisons.

Are the benchmarks an exact percentile?

No. Benchmarks are general performance ranges drawn from cited sources. They are useful as broad guidance but should not be treated as percentile rankings.

Will this help with recruiting?

This site does not predict, guarantee, or determine recruiting outcomes. It can give you a quick directional estimate to share with a coach or training partner. Talk to a qualified coach for individualized advice.
