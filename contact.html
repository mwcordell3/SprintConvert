Email

For corrections, source suggestions, or accessibility issues, contact: sprintconvert@outlook.com

There is no contact form on this site. Email is the only contact channel in v1, by design — we do not collect submissions, store messages on a server, or use a third-party form service.

What's most useful to send

  • Corrections: The page URL, the specific number or sentence that's wrong, and what it should say.
  • Source suggestions: A link to a public reference that supports a benchmark range we don't yet publish (for example, a women's-specific 40-yard recruiting source). We will only add benchmarks with strong cited sources.
  • Accessibility issues: What page, what assistive technology and browser, what action you took, and what happened versus what you expected.
  • Calculator bugs: The inputs you used, the result you got, and the result you expected.

What we can't help with

  • Recruiting decisions — talk to a coach or recruiting professional.
  • Medical questions — talk to a qualified medical professional.
  • Official sprint times for a specific event — contact the timing company or event organizer.
