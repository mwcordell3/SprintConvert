Baseball 60 Yard Dash Calculator

Estimate your baseball 60-yard dash time, average speed, and a general recruiting tier. Educational only — not a recruiting service or showcase result.

The 60-yard dash is the standard baseball speed test for showcases and high school / college recruiting. Times are usually run from a standing start with a "rocker" technique. Showcase timing varies — some events use laser systems, others hand stopwatches, and surfaces differ between turf and grass.

This calculator returns an estimated 60-yard time, conversions to other distances, average speed, and a general recruiting tier sourced from cited public guidelines.

About the benchmark tier

Our baseball 60-yard ranges follow NCSA recruiting guidelines. The displayed tier is a broad range, not a percentile, and a single time will fluctuate run-to-run by 0.05–0.20 seconds depending on the surface, footwear, and timing setup.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 6.8 sixty good for college baseball?

A 6.80 falls inside the cited NCSA 'advanced' range for high school and college recruiting. Surface, technique, and timing equipment can shift the result, so treat the tier as broad context.

Should I use a standing or rocker start?

Pick the start type the testing facility uses. The calculator's standing-start option treats both similarly. If you used a flying-build-up, choose Rolling start to keep the conversion honest.

Does this predict whether I will be recruited?

No. This calculator does not predict, guarantee, or otherwise determine recruiting outcomes. Talk to coaches and recruiting professionals for guidance specific to your situation.
